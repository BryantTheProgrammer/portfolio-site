About Bryant

A builder, teacher, and systems thinker.

My career has moved through education, hands-on STEM, computer science, and industrial software. The common thread is helping people understand complex systems and turning that understanding into something useful.

The throughline

I have always worked at the boundary between people and systems.

Before working in industrial software, I taught music and worked with students through robotics, LEGO, and Rubik's Cube activities. Teaching shaped how I approach technical work: listen first, explain clearly, and build something that the person using it can actually trust.

Computer science gave me the formal foundation to design software, work with data, and reason about systems. In manufacturing, that foundation became practical: applications have to work during production, communicate with equipment, and remain supportable after they are commissioned.

Today I build and support SCADA, MES, and IIoT applications with Ignition, Python, SQL, and industrial integrations. I am comfortable moving between an operator conversation, a programming specification, a control system, and a leadership presentation.

How I work

Practical, curious, and accountable.
